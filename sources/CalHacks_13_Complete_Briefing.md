# Cal Hacks 13.0 — ramp-up briefing

Researched October 23, 2026, Pacific time. This is a snapshot of the event as it begins. Sources: attendee Gmail sponsor/logistics announcements, the Cal Hacks 13.0 Slack workspace, Devpost, the current Hive site, and sponsor starter packs linked by organizers. Amounts below distinguish cash, gift cards, USDC, hardware, and service credits. No credits have been redeemed and no messages or applications have been sent.

**Best starting decision:** choose a real user and one demonstrable workflow, then choose the sponsor track that rewards it. For enterprise/finance agents, start with Hyphenate and Microsoft; for a defined community need, Anthropic; for live web intelligence, Bright Data or Apify; for voice, Cartesia; for multiplayer/shared state, SpacetimeDB. These are recommendations based on the published criteria, not predictions of winning odds.

**Essential bookmarks:** [Live schedule](https://hive.hackberkeley.org/schedule), [live prizes](https://hive.hackberkeley.org/prizes), [starter packs](https://hive.hackberkeley.org/resources), [Hacker Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RQrueC9wm5u8uxtWSC2gadIchp05hVayhGUrIjxT9dE/edit), [Devpost](https://cal-hacks-13-0.devpost.com/), [mentor queue](https://hive.hackberkeley.org/mentorship-queue), [Slack announcements](https://cal-hacks-13-0.slack.com/archives/C0C7TCNCR1N).

**Source conflicts:** prefer a current, explicit sponsor announcement for its reward and criteria; use Hive for current event times. Some conflicts remain unresolved below. Devpost's dollar fields sometimes represent the value of hardware or credits: do not interpret its headline prize pool as cash available to winners.

**6. Submission and build decisions to protect now**

- All implementation must happen during the event. Make a fresh event project; pre-event ideation is permitted.
- Add every teammate on Devpost; maximum four members. The Guide recommends an initial Devpost draft by Saturday night.
- Include image, source repository, working demo and clear video. Sponsors may evaluate asynchronously, so a live demo alone is insufficient preparation.
- General Devpost pitch format is three minutes plus two minutes Q&A, while Guide recommends a two-minute pitch and some sponsors explicitly require two minutes. Prepare a concise two-minute core and adapt to the actual judge's format.
- Sunday table assignments come after submission; move by 10:30 AM and stay through 12:30 PM. Guide asks the whole team to be present; a newer organizer response permits judging without a teammate who must leave, but no early judging. Confirm exceptions rather than planning around them.
- General evaluation: real-world usefulness, purposeful UX, working functionality, originality and engineering depth. The best evidence is a narrow end-to-end flow, one real user's pain, an observable successful outcome, and one handled failure.
- Track-specific extras matter: Figma stage log (maximum 200 words per stage), slides and maximum three-minute video; CREAO agent ID/run share link; Coinbase architecture and builder feedback; Factory README explaining runtime SDK use; Cartesia setup/features/limitations; Commit supplemental form.
